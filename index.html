Nine holes, nine stat leaderboards. Each hole asks for the player at a specific rank. Type a name and swing. The closer you are to the right spot, the better your score.

  • −3Hole in one: the exact player.
  • −2 / −1Eagle 1–5 spots off · Birdie 6–10 off
  • EPar 11–20 spots off
  • +1 / +2Bogey 21–30 off · Double bogey 31–40 off
  • +3Triple bogey 41+ off.
  • 3 strikesA name that isn't on the leaderboard is a strike, and you swing again. Three strikes or Give up = splash (+3).
  • TiesBroken by total spots off (a splash counts as 100), then fewest strikes.

Everyone who picks the same sports gets the same course today.

There's no list of names to pick from, and the game never tells you who's on a list. Type the full name; small typos are forgiven.

Unlimited plays random courses, as many as you want. Those rounds don't count toward the daily leaderboard.
